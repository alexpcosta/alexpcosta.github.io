How to play

Objective. Be the last player able to place a domino. The player who cannot make a legal move loses.

Rules. Two players take turns placing a single domino, which covers two orthogonally adjacent empty squares of the 8×8 board.

  • Vertical (Blue) may only place dominoes vertically — covering a square and the one directly below it.
  • Horizontal (Red) may only place dominoes horizontally — covering a square and the one directly to its right.

Tap a square to place your piece. Vertical anchors at the top square; Horizontal anchors at the left square. The game ends the moment the player whose turn it is has no legal move — and that player loses.

Material: 1 square 8×8 board, plenty of dominoes (~32). Human vs Human.

Vertical wins!
